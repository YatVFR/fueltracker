Car Fuel Tracker — Fuel Lasted Dashboard Update

  Fuel Lasted

- With 2 or more refuels:
  Main value = days between the latest two refuels.
  Subtext = current tank age in days.
- With 1 refuel:
  Main value = days since that refuel.

  Shows —.

Existing fuel records are unchanged.
