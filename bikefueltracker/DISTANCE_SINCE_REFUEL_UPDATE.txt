Bike Fuel Tracker — Distance Since Refuel Update

New dashboard metric:
  Distance Since Refuel

Calculation:
  Latest/current odometer minus odometer recorded at the most recent refuel.

If no newer odometer reading is available after the last refuel, the card shows:
  0 km
  update odometer on next entry

After updating the app files:
1. fueltrackers start
2. Reload the tracker in Safari
3. Open the Home Screen app once
4. Confirm the new dashboard card appears
5. fueltrackers stop

PWA offline operation is retained.
